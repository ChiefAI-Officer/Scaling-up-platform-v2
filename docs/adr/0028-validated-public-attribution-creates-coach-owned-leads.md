# Validated public attribution creates coach-owned durable leads

A completed Public Campaign submission becomes a durable coach-owned Public lead only when its attributed link resolves to a verified active Referring coach and the retention and abuse-policy boundaries are healthy. A Public lead is that existing completed submission classified by a nullable stable coach owner and attribution snapshot—not a second lead record—so identity, result, retention, and deletion state have one source of truth. Ownership is snapshotted to that stable coach account rather than recomputed from a mutable email, so an email change cannot orphan or transfer leads; deactivation removes portal access without deleting ownership, and reactivation of the same account restores it. That coach may revisit the full frozen Results report through the authenticated Report access gate and export only the lead's name, email, submission time, and assessment name; scores and answers remain inside the individually accessed report. Submitted contact data permits one-to-one follow-up about that assessment and its results only; newsletter enrollment or unrelated marketing requires a separate affirmative opt-in outside this scope. Ordinary list access emits structured metrics, while each report view and export generation/download requires a fail-closed durable audit with actor, scope, and provenance. Admin/STAFF retain oversight, while unattributed, invalidly attributed, or policy/limiter-degraded submissions remain Scaling Up-owned. Historical submissions are backfilled only from an owner-approved dry-run mapping with frozen identity evidence; current normalized-email uniqueness alone is not historical proof. The migration records matched, unmatched, and ambiguous counts, sends no email, and leaves every unresolved row Scaling Up-owned. This chooses durable lead ownership over one-time email notification, so coach access is derived from validated attribution rather than ownership of the shared Public Campaign, without creating a durable anonymous results URL or a bulk assessment-data export. Because the current public consent copy omits retention, the coach-facing lead surface must remain dark until Scaling Up approves a retention period and the product's deletion behavior and participant-facing wording match it.
