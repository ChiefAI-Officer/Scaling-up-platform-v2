# Jeff's Esperto Report-Fidelity Direction — Meeting Evidence

**Question:** Did Jeff explicitly say in the August 13 and/or August 20, 2026
meetings that Scaling Up reports should look as close as possible to Esperto?

**Finding:** **No blanket instruction is supported.** Jeff explicitly approved
an Esperto-like **Scaling Up Full question-detail flow** on August 13, but he also
said the broader Peers view did not need to mimic the whole Esperto design. The
only exact “look as close” statement concerned the separate public mini-quiz
lead-magnet result. On August 20 he expressly accepted report data being “laid
out different” and prioritized correct report logic and content over presentation
glitz.

## August 13: explicit SU Full approval, narrowly scoped

At **3:56**, while discussing the detailed **You versus Peers** presentation for
each Scaling Up Full question, Jeff said:

> “I kind of like this flow here a lot. If we could, I'm not opposed to just
> blatantly stealing this. So I like the question, the two bars, and then the
> answer under that bar. I really like that. So if we can mimic that, that would
> be my goal.”

Source: August 13 transcript, lines **53–69**, especially **65–66**;
[recording at 3:56](https://fathom.video/share/c1JY5DVS2d5qvyfixGmMJYet-oFNSG-4?timestamp=236).

This is direct approval for the Esperto-like sequence **question → two bars →
answer beneath**. It supports Layout A if Layout A is the option that preserves
that detail flow.

It is not approval to clone the entire Esperto report. Earlier, at **1:40**, Jeff
said of the broader vertical Peers view:

> “I'm not necessarily in love with this view anyways, so I don't need to mimic
> the whole thing, just the idea of it.”

He then chose the upper-left paired micro-bar alternative and asked that “me” and
“peer” be immediately identifiable. Source: August 13 transcript, lines **19–48**,
especially **22–30** and **41–48**;
[recording at 1:40](https://fathom.video/share/c1JY5DVS2d5qvyfixGmMJYet-oFNSG-4?timestamp=100).

### The exact “look as close” quote was about the mini-quiz

At **11:58**, Jeff said:

> “So from this particular one, ultimately, I want it to look as close to this
> as I can.”

The preceding and following exchange identifies “this particular one” as the
public **mini-quiz marketing lead magnet**, not the Scaling Up Full report. Jeff
described its purpose as capturing a visitor's name and routing them to books or
other offers. Source: August 13 transcript, lines **138–194**, especially
**144–151**, **174–188**, and **193–194**;
[recording at 11:58](https://fathom.video/share/c1JY5DVS2d5qvyfixGmMJYet-oFNSG-4?timestamp=718).

Therefore, applying that sentence to every report—or specifically to the whole
Scaling Up Full report—would be an inference the primary source does not support.

## August 20: different layout accepted; correctness came first

At **14:24**, while comparing the platform and Esperto reports side by side, Jeff
said of the central report data:

> “report data, same, same, laid out, different, whatever. I'm okay with that.”

His request in that exchange was for editable intro/preface and call-to-action
sections around the report data, not Esperto-identical central layout. Source:
August 20 transcript, lines **156–187**, especially **162–181**;
[recording at 14:24](https://fathom.video/share/BG18iJ5yYy28yRFYVYoode9hJXCYpYAy?timestamp=864).

At **17:51**, Jeff set the order of operations: finish the Scaling Up report's
levels, question answers, and “core functionality” first, while deferring the
preface and CTA as “glitz” and “marketing crap.” He wanted the report “done right”
so it could be shown to a coach. Source: August 20 transcript, lines **216–241**,
especially **222–223**;
[recording at 17:51](https://fathom.video/share/BG18iJ5yYy28yRFYVYoode9hJXCYpYAy?timestamp=1071).

At **3:21**, he also asked to reproduce the “same kind of technology” after
testing answer values 1–10. That is evidence for behavioral fidelity, not an
instruction for pixel-identical visual styling. Source: August 20 transcript,
lines **41–61**, especially **48–55**;
[recording at 3:21](https://fathom.video/share/BG18iJ5yYy28yRFYVYoode9hJXCYpYAy?timestamp=201).

At **19:57–21:56**, Jeff learned that Peer values could change with the selected
organizational phase and concluded, “I'm glad we did that then” and “continue
working on this report.” This confirms the importance of correct phase-dependent
Peer behavior, but contains no visual-fidelity direction. Source: August 20
transcript, lines **228–241**;
[recording at 19:57](https://fathom.video/share/BG18iJ5yYy28yRFYVYoode9hJXCYpYAy?timestamp=1197).

## Decision implication for Layout A

Proceeding with **Layout A** is evidence-backed **for the Scaling Up Full
question-detail flow**, provided it keeps:

1. the question;
2. clearly labeled You and Peers bars; and
3. the answer/feedback directly beneath them.

The evidence does **not** require the rest of the report to imitate Esperto as
closely as possible. Layout and brand treatment may differ where the core data,
phase-aware behavior, readability, and question-detail flow remain correct.
